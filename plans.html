How pricing works

Monthly pricing depends on the number of people and computers in your office. You receive a written quote before anything starts.

Upgrades, migrations and new-device setup are quoted separately as projects.

Questions

Is there a long-term contract?

Terms are written into your service agreement before you sign.

What counts as helpdesk support?

Requests from your staff about computers, accounts, email and software. Your agreement lists what is covered and what is billed separately.

Can I add or remove computers?

Yes. Your monthly price follows the number of people and computers you have.
