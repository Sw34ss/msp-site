Services

Everything a small office usually needs from IT, in plain terms.

Helpdesk and remote support

When something isn’t working, email us. Each request is tracked so nothing gets lost, and most problems are fixed in a remote session.

  • Sign-in and password problems
  • Slow or misbehaving computers
  • Printer, Wi-Fi and software questions

Microsoft 365 and email

We manage the accounts your team relies on every day, so people get what they need without waiting.

  • Adding and removing users and mailboxes
  • Shared mailboxes, files and Teams
  • License checks so you’re not paying for unused seats

New devices and new staff

A new hire should be productive on day one, and a departing employee’s access should be closed the same day.

  • Computers set up the same way every time
  • Accounts created with the right access
  • Offboarding checklist when someone leaves

Monitoring and updates

We watch your computers in the background and install updates on a schedule, so small problems get fixed before they become outages.

  • Alerts for offline computers, low disk space and failing services
  • Operating system and software updates
  • A short monthly summary of what we found and fixed

Security basics

Most attacks succeed because of missed basics. We cover them first.

  • Protection software on every computer
  • Multi-factor sign-in for email and key accounts
  • Plain-English guidance on scam emails and passwords

Backup and recovery planning

A backup you’ve never tested is a guess. We help you decide what to protect and confirm that it can be restored.

  • What needs backing up, and how often
  • Regular checks that backups are running
  • Test restores so there are no surprises

Upgrades and migrations

Changing systems is where offices lose the most time. We plan the move around your schedule.

  • New computers and replacing old ones
  • Moving email or files to the cloud
  • A written plan and a rollback option for each change

IT check-ups and planning

A one-time review is a good way to start. You get a clear picture of what you have and what to do first.

  • Inventory of computers, accounts and subscriptions
  • A short list of risks, ranked by importance
  • A realistic budget for the next 12 months

What we don’t do: structured cabling and camera or alarm installation. If you need them, we’ll point you to a licensed contractor.
